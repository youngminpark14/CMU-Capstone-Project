# 0047. An operator can ask for one live episode on one market, with a Bitcoin series for demonstrations

`pmfa ask SERIES` runs one shadow-mode episode now, on one market, saves its sources, and waits for the person's decision.

## Context

The owner wants to give a live demonstration from a terminal: one episode on one market that comes up often, a place to read the sources it used, a YES or NO from the agent, and the last word on the trade. The followed series do not allow that. Fed decisions and monthly CPI open an event about once a month, a shadow pass forecasts whatever its triggers find, and an event has from five to twenty-six markets, all forecast together. Kalshi's `KXBTCD` series ("Bitcoin price Above/below") opens an event every hour, settles on CF Benchmarks' Bitcoin Real-Time Index, and lists close to two hundred strikes per hourly event; the strikes near the current price trade heavily.

The specification's trigger list names a new document, a price move and a schedule tick. It does not name an operator's request. Its evidence is official sources only, and news feeds are out of scope for v1 (decision [0011](0011-official-sources-only.md)), though the evidence port was built to take them.

## Decision

- **One command, not a fifth run mode.** `pmfa ask` opens the shadow session with review on ([session.py](../../src/pmfa/live/session.py)): live production prices read-only, the paper executor, the shadow state database, and the same review path as `pmfa review --mode shadow`, whose decision code both commands now share (`pmfa.live.session.decide`). The steps are in [live/ask.py](../../src/pmfa/live/ask.py).
- **A schedule-kind trigger stands for the request.** The trigger is `schedule:<event>:asked:<time>` with `kind` `schedule` and the detail "asked by the operator". A trigger that names its event skips the match step and its model call (decision [0017](0017-named-event-triggers-skip-monitor.md)). No trigger kind or record field was added.
- **The event and the market.** The series' open event that closes soonest among those closing 10 to 60 minutes from now, so the episode takes the fast path and fits the 90-second cap; then the market whose YES mid is nearest 0.50. `--market` names one instead. When no event fits, nothing is read from the evidence sources and no model is called.
- **One market per episode.** A wrapper around the Exchange port ([world/focus.py](../../src/pmfa/world/focus.py)) returns the chosen event with only the chosen market; every other call is passed through. The existing ladder outcome space then has two outcomes, and the paper executor and every step read through the wrapper.
- **Sources.** A series may list `feeds` (read while it is followed) and a `spot_product`. For `KXBTCD`, Coinbase's public `BTC-USD` price is read into one document ([spot.py](../../src/pmfa/world/evidence/spot.py)) and counts as a primary origin for the `crypto` category; three crypto news feeds are secondary. The snapshot says that the settlement index is an average over several exchanges, so Coinbase alone can differ from it. A new snapshot replaces the earlier ones in the retrieval index (the evidence store keeps every one), because a check run three times in a row filled every retrieval slot with snapshots minutes apart and left no room for the news.
- **Every proposal waits, and is decided in the run.** The session pauses every approve-tier proposal, and routing gives the auto tier only at the autonomous stage, so `pmfa ask` refuses to run when the shadow stage is autonomous rather than lower any limit. It also refuses, before reading anything, when shadow mode is halted or the live gateway has no API key. The person's answer is taken in the run: a resize out of range is asked again, and Ctrl-C or Ctrl-D at the question rejects. A proposal left for later could not be approved: `pmfa review` runs on the loaded configuration, where `KXBTCD` is off, and the gate refuses an unfollowed series.
- **Its sources stay its own.** The live modes store what they poll in `data/live-corpus/`, never in the frozen backtest corpus, where a live document would become a news trigger in the next backtest. A series' own feeds and spot price are left out of other categories' retrieval inside the retrieval call ([origins.py](../../src/pmfa/knowledge/origins.py) `other_category_sources`), so crypto news never reaches the official-sources Fed and CPI episodes. Earlier snapshots leave the index even when a new one cannot be read, so a failed read means no price evidence, not a stale one.
- **Switched off by default.** `KXBTCD` is a configured series with `enabled = false`. `pmfa ask` switches it on in memory for its one run, so backtests, `pmfa data build`, `pmfa shadow` and `pmfa demo` never follow it.
- **What is saved.** `runs/ask/<time>-<market>/` holds one Markdown file per kept source with the used passages marked, `answer.md` and `episode.json`, whatever the exit.

## Consequences

The owner can show the whole pipeline on live data in about a minute for a few cents of model calls, and keep what it read. The Bitcoin series is a demonstration, not an evaluation: an hourly price market is close to a coin flip around the current price, and the agent is not expected to beat it. Its forecasts enter the shadow ledger under their own category, `crypto`, so they do not mix with the Fed and CPI calibration. A Coinbase price is not an official publisher's release in the specification's sense; it is primary only for this series' category, and only because the market settles on an exchange price index. The outcome resolver now builds a resolved episode's outcome space from the markets the episode forecast, so the pruning audit of a one-market episode is computed on its two outcomes. Shadow mode no longer reads the frozen corpus as background: it starts from what its own polls have stored, which is what the specification's modes table gives it ("live official feeds").
